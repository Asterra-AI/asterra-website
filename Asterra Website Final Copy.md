Oct 6, 2026 · @Yar

Final copy for asterra.ca, in page order. The home page is 12 sections, each a headline, one line and a visual; detail lives on the other pages. This replaces the earlier Full Content draft.

## Ground rules

One job: get an owner from "what is this?" to "build my demo" with no meeting and no sales call.

**The funnel every page serves**

1. **Hear it answer.** The toy on the home page. No sign-up.
2. **Build my demo.** Three fields. A number to call, on their real menu, within 48 hours.
3. **Two weeks free.** Live on their real line.
4. **One flat price.** Month to month, set by how busy the phone is.

Every page ends on "Hear it answer" or "Build my demo". Nothing says "Book a call".

**Copy rules**

- A headline is one short sentence, ending in a period. One line under it, never a paragraph.
- No labels, tags or pills above headlines.
- Owners' words: calls, orders, tables, rush, counter, kitchen. Canadian spelling.
- Languages are always "20+ languages". Never name one in marketing copy (the toy's sample prompts are the exception).
- No testimonials wall, no logo wall. One real client, shown properly.
- Never: revolutionary, cutting-edge, game-changer, seamless, leverage, unlock, supercharge, effortless, AI-powered.

**What we never claim**

- That we built our own language model, speech recognition or voices. The system around them is ours: flows, integrations, safeguards, dashboard, testing.
- Integrations or features that aren't live. "Coming soon" or leave it out.
- Client names, quotes or numbers without written permission.
- Any stat without a source, or any total that shows how small we are.

## How it works page (/how-it-works)

**H1:** What happens when someone calls.

**Line:** From the first ring to your dashboard.

### The call

A left-to-right diagram, one short line per step:

1. **It picks up.** Your number forwards to Asterra. No ringing out, no hold.
2. **It listens.** Leading speech providers, chosen for each language.
3. **It understands.** Works out what the caller wants and follows your rules.
4. **It checks your systems.** Menu, prices and availability come from your POS and your book.
5. **It answers.** A natural voice, in the caller's language.
6. **It acts.** The order goes to your POS, the table to your book, or the call to your staff.
7. **It reports.** Recording, transcript and outcome appear on your dashboard.

### No awkward pauses.

It replies in about a second, knows when the caller has finished, and stops when it's interrupted.

### It doesn't make things up.

- Menu and prices come from your POS. If it's not on your menu, it's not for sale.
- Totals are worked out by our system, never guessed.
- Every order is read back before it's placed.
- When it isn't sure, it says so and transfers.

### Tested before your customers hear it.

- Every change is built and tested away from your live line and your POS.
- We make real test calls: orders, bookings, edge cases, every language you use.
- We test with you in person, with real orders printing in your kitchen.
- In the first weeks, we review your calls every day.

### If something goes wrong, a person picks up.

- Many calls at once, so there's never a busy signal.
- If anything fails, the call goes to your staff instead of dropping.
- From the dashboard: pause orders, switch to questions only, or turn it off.

### Your data stays yours.

Encrypted in transit and at rest. Each restaurant's data is kept separate. No card numbers, ever. \[Security & privacy →\]
